Mission briefing · FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it cost anything to enquire?

No — enquiring is completely free, and it includes a free consultation. We'll discuss your child's goals and recommend the right tutor and format before you commit to anything.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We'll reply by email to arrange your free consultation. From there we match your child with a Band 6 tutor in their subject and organise the first lesson once you're happy to go ahead.

What year levels do you support?

Years 7 through 12 — including students preparing for senior assessments and final exams.

Which subjects can you help with?

All major school subjects — English, Mathematics, the Sciences, and Humanities subjects like History, Legal Studies, Business Studies, and Economics. Every student is matched with a tutor who achieved a Band 6 in that specific subject.

Do you offer one-on-one or group lessons?

Both. Personalised one-on-one tutoring, or small online group classes for students who enjoy a collaborative format. Not sure which suits best? That's exactly what the free consultation is for.

How are students matched with a tutor?

We review the student's year level, subject, and goals, then pair them with the tutor — or class option — that fits best. Every tutor teaches a subject they achieved a Band 6 in.
